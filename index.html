Questions

FAQ

Do I need gym access or can I train at home? +

Both work. During the application process you tell us what equipment you have access to — full gym, home gym, or no equipment at all. Your program is built around exactly what you have. No excuses, no barriers.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this right for me? +

Absolutely. Some of our best results come from beginners because they have the most room to grow and no bad habits to unlearn. We start where you are — not where you think you should be.

How is this different from following YouTube workouts? +

YouTube gives you generic programs designed for an imaginary average person. We design your program for your body, your goals, your schedule, your equipment, and your food preferences — then we adjust it every single week based on how you're actually responding. That's coaching, not content.

What does the weekly check-in involve? +

Every Sunday you fill out a short form: your weight, how training felt, compliance, sleep, and energy. You submit a workout log and optionally a form-check video. By Monday we review everything and update your program or nutrition accordingly. Takes you 5 minutes. Changes everything.

What if I don't see results? +

In 90 days of consistent, coached effort, you will see results — because we track every variable and adjust in real time. Results require adherence. Our job is to make adherence as easy as possible. If something isn't working, we change it. That's the point of having a coach.

How do I pay? Is it secure? +

We use Razorpay — India's most trusted payment gateway — accepting UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, and EMI. Payment links are sent directly to you after your discovery call. Zero hidden fees.

Can I switch plans later? +

Yes. Many clients start with the Group Cohort and upgrade to 1:1 Elite after experiencing the coaching quality. Just reach out and we'll sort it out seamlessly.
